New package: the AgentaOS checkout on your own page — inline or overlay. `loadAgentaOS()` loads the checkout script once (SSR-safe), typed options and events (`checkout.loaded`, `checkout.completed`, `checkout.closed`, `checkout.error`), and `@agentaos/checkout/react` with `<AgentaOSCheckout>` and `useAgentaOSCheckout()`.
